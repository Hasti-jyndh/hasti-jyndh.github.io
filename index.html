About

I'm a master's student in Information Science & Technology at The University of Osaka, where I work in the Integrated Media Environment lab with Prof. Uranishi as a MEXT Scholar. My research asks how a mobile robot can adjust its own planner on the fly when people and obstacles keep moving.

I came to robotics through aerospace engineering at Sharif University of Technology, where my bachelor's thesis put real-time visual SLAM on a physical robot and won the Best Bachelor's Thesis Award. Since then I've worked across security engineering and data science, most recently building a ranking model that went into production at C Channel in Tokyo.
